# Assets

Nothing is hotlinked and nothing needs a licence:

- `public/videos/*.webm`: lesson clips generated by `npm run videos` (animated title slides drawn on a canvas and encoded in the browser). They are placeholders for real lesson footage.
- `public/thumbs/*.svg`: hand-written course thumbnails.

To replace a placeholder clip with real footage, drop a short, compressed `.webm` or `.mp4` into `public/videos/`, keep the same filename (or update `videoUrl` in `db/demo-data.ts` and re-seed), and set the matching `durationSec`. Check the licence of any footage you add and note it here.

Not included, would need real assets: captions or subtitles for the video lessons (the generated clips are silent).
